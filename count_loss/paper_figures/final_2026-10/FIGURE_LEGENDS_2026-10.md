# Figure legends (draft, 2026-10-02)

Model names describe the loss set. CG: CycleGAN (adversarial and cycle losses; H&E drawn independently of the SRS patch). CG+M: CG with paired-location sampling and a macro-architecture loss. CG+M+A and CG+M+C are alternatives that both branch from CG+M: CG+M+A adds a nuclear-area loss, CG+M+C adds a local nucleus-count loss. Each model was trained three times (seeds 42, 7, 123) for 30 epochs with identical data, splits, input channel (SRS) and settings.

Common evaluation, unless stated otherwise: held-out evaluation cores (17 cores, 1038 patches of 100 um; none used to select models or epochs). Nuclei counted with StarDist (2D_versatile_he) on the rendered RGB output and on the adjacent real H&E section after the same H&E round trip used for the generated images (stain deconvolution and re-rendering). Per patch, the generated count is the mean over the checkpoints of epochs 27 to 30. Model values are means over the three seeds. 95% confidence intervals: core-cluster bootstrap (1000 resamples; cores resampled jointly across seeds). Density classes use the real count of the adjacent section: sparse < 33, intermediate 33 to 55, dense >= 56 nuclei per patch. r: per-patch Pearson correlation of generated and real counts. Slope: log-log slope of generated on real count (1 = proportional). Disc: median generated/real ratio in dense patches divided by that in sparse patches (1 = no compression). Model colours and marker shapes are fixed across all figures.

## Figure 1. Study design.
(a) One tissue-microarray core (B-1) imaged label-free in four channels (SHG, SRS, CARS, TPEF) and the adjacent section stained with H&E. Only the SRS channel is the model input. The two sections are parallel, not identical: there is no cell-level correspondence. (b) The H&E section is registered onto the SRS grid (cohort scale 1.02317 H&E px per SRS px, left-right mirror, rigid refinement; Supplementary Figs S1, S2), cut into 100 um patches at the same location, and decomposed into haematoxylin (H) and eosin (E) channels with a calibrated stain matrix. (c) A generator translates SRS into H and E; training is unpaired (CycleGAN: two generators, two discriminators, cycle consistency). The four models form a branch: CG, then CG+M (paired sampling and macro loss), then either CG+M+A (area loss) or CG+M+C (count loss). (d) The count loss: a frozen nucleus counter (Supplementary Fig. S3) reads the generated H channel in a 2 x 2 grid of 50 um cells, and each cell count is compared with the real count of the adjacent section; there is no patch-total term. Evaluation is identical for every model (box).

## Figure 2. Example outputs.
Rows: one sparse, one intermediate, one dense and one stromal patch. Columns: SRS input, adjacent real H&E, and the output of each model. Numbers are StarDist nuclei per 100 um patch (real: adjacent section; generated: the evaluation counts). All generated images are seed 42, epoch 30, for every model (a fixed rule). Seed 42 is CG's weakest seed: it does not follow the SRS structure (r -0.10; Supplementary Fig. S6), whereas CG seeds 7 and 123 do. Patches were chosen for image quality from density-stratified candidates by a fixed rule on the real section only (registered area, focus, no dark artefacts, SRS-H&E outline agreement), never by model output; an unselected set drawn at random within each class is shown in Supplementary Fig. S9. Real and generated images come from adjacent sections, so cell-level agreement is not expected. Scale bar 20 um.

## Supplementary Figure S5. Detector robustness.
(a) r, log-log slope and disc under StarDist (reference), CellViT-SAM-H and InstanSeg, seed values summarised as mean with 95% CI. All three detectors give the same model order on every metric. (b) Paired differences under each detector. The pre-registered contrast, CG+M+C minus CG+M+A, holds on r under all three detectors (StarDist +0.09 [0.04, 0.14], CellViT-SAM-H +0.06 [0.01, 0.11], InstanSeg +0.07 [0.03, 0.12]); the slope and disc gains are smaller under the independent detectors and their CIs include 0 (slope +0.02 [-0.02, 0.07] and +0.02 [-0.01, 0.05]; disc +0.05 [-0.01, 0.10] and +0.02 [-0.02, 0.05]). (c) The H&E round trip on the 1038 real patches: median round-trip/original count 1.000 (StarDist), 0.966 (CellViT-SAM-H), 1.030 (InstanSeg). The second detectors were chosen from five candidates on pre-registered criteria (agreement with StarDist on real patches, round-trip stability, behaviour on pale generated images, cost).

## Supplementary Figure S8. Why a local count loss (design rationale).
(a) In CG+M+A, the area loss acts on the haematoxylin-positive area (generated H channel at the calibrated nuclear threshold). Its generated/real ratio (squares) falls less steeply with density than the generated/real nucleus count (diamonds): 0.90 / 0.83 / 0.70 against 1.19 / 0.85 / 0.58 (sparse / intermediate / dense; mean over seeds, epochs 27 to 30, 95% CI). The area signal is compressed relative to the count deficit and has the opposite sign in sparse tissue. (b) Share of a count loss carried by a patch-total term and by the per-cell term for a 40% undercount: at 12.5 um cells (0.8 real nuclei per cell) the patch total carries 93%, at 50 um cells (12.3 nuclei per cell) 44%. (c) Agreement between the SRS section and the adjacent H&E section (architecture correlation, mean over 75 registered cores) grows with cell size, from 0.15 at 6 um to 0.40 at 100 um; the two loss cell sizes are marked.

## Methods sentences (draft)
- Primary baseline: generated counts are compared with real counts after the same H&E round trip (stain deconvolution with the calibrated matrix and re-rendering) that every generated image undergoes; StarDist loses no nuclei in this round trip (median ratio 1.000). Values differ by at most 0.01 from the original-RGB baseline.
- Second detectors: CellViT-SAM-H and InstanSeg were chosen from five candidate detectors on criteria fixed in advance (agreement with StarDist on real patches, round-trip stability, behaviour on pale generated images, cost).
- Claim wording: the gain of CG+M+C over CG+M+A in correlation is detector-robust; the slope and dense/sparse gains are seen with StarDist and are smaller with independent detectors.
